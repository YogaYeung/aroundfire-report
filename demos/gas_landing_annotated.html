Straight answers

Fire-Ban FAQ

The questions people in restricted counties actually ask — answered honestly.

Can I use this during a Stage 1 fire restriction?
In many areas, yes. Stage 1 restrictions typically ban open wood and charcoal fires but allow propane devices with a shut-off valve, used a safe distance (commonly 3 ft) from flammable material. A propane burner gives you a controllable flame you can cut off instantly. Rules differ by county, park and agency. Always check your local restrictions before use — some jurisdictions and public lands restrict propane too.
What about Stage 2 restrictions — is propane still allowed?
Stage 2 is stricter, but many Stage 2 orders still permit propane/liquid-fuel devices with a shut-off valve because the flame can be extinguished immediately, unlike a wood fire. That's exactly why the propane configuration exists. This is not a guarantee for your location. Check your local restrictions before use — read the specific order for your county, park or agency, since some Stage 2 bans do include propane.
Is this legal everywhere / is it "fire-ban proof"?
No product is legal everywhere, and we won't claim it is. Some public lands — for example North Cascades National Park — restrict propane fire pits specifically. What we can say honestly: propane devices with a shut-off valve are permitted under many Stage 1 & Stage 2 restrictions when used 3 ft from flammable material. Always check your local fire restrictions before use. The rule that applies to you is the one posted by your county, park or land-management agency — not this page.
Does it come with a propane tank?
No — the propane configuration runs off a standard propane tank you supply, so you can swap and refill anywhere. The burner and shut-off valve are part of the table setup. Follow the included instructions for safe tank connection and clearance, and check your local restrictions before use.
Can I still cook a full meal on it like the wood version?
Yes. It's the same Gather table with grill surface around all four sides — you're feeding the same crew, just with a propane flame in the center well instead of wood or charcoal. Great for burn-ban season when open fires aren't an option. Cooking clearances and safe-use guidance are in the product instructions; check your local restrictions before use.
